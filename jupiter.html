Gas giant

Explore Jupiter.

Jupiter is a hydrogen-and-helium gas giant with colorful cloud bands, powerful jet streams, and enormous storms including the Great Red Spot. It has no solid surface on which to stand; pressure and temperature rise dramatically deeper inside. Jupiter also has an intense radiation environment and a large system of moons.
