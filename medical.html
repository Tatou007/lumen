One suit for every world.

Every planet in the store has its own astronaut suit concept. Each design takes its colors and mood from its world, and its equipment list explains what that environment demands.

These are conceptual suit designs for educational and planning purposes, not certified or flight-ready equipment. Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus and Neptune have no solid surface, so their suits are speculative remote-atmosphere concepts.
