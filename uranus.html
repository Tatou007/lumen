Explore Uranus.

Uranus is an ice giant with an atmosphere dominated by hydrogen and helium, plus methane that absorbs red light and contributes to its blue-green color. Its axis is tilted by about 98 degrees, producing extreme seasons. The upper atmosphere is frigid, and the planet has faint rings and a complex magnetic field.
